📖 Nutrition glossary
Calories
Energy from food. Fat loss needs a small, steady calorie deficit.
Protein
Builds/preserves muscle while losing fat. Aim for a good source at every meal.
Carbohydrates
Main energy source. Complex carbs (oats, millets) digest slower than simple sugars.
Fat
Needed in moderation for hormones — don't eliminate, just portion it.
Fiber
Keeps you full longer, aids digestion — found in veg, whole grains, fruit skins.
BMR
Basal Metabolic Rate — calories your body burns at rest just to function.
TDEE
Total Daily Energy Expenditure — BMR plus activity. Eating below this creates the deficit.
Deficit
Eating less than you burn — the core mechanism behind fat loss.
